Ask us a question ↗
Is solar suitable for my property?

It depends on factors such as roof space, orientation, shading, energy use and local requirements.

Can I explore battery storage too?

Yes. Battery storage may be worth considering alongside solar, depending on your energy needs, budget and usage patterns.

How do I get started?

Use the enquiry form on our contact page to share a few details. SolarShop can then follow up to discuss possible next steps.
